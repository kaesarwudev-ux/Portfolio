LocalFirst-Flashcards - Pre-Alpha

A desktop flashcard and notes app built with Tauri. Study offline. Supports Ollama for offline work along with other common AI providers. May have lots of bugs as it is in Pre-Alpha.

v0.3.0 Windows x64 macOS Apple Silicon Android
Windows (.exe) macOS (.dmg) Android (.apk)
Android blocked the install?
  1. Chrome blocked the APK download — tap the download notification, then tap Keep or Download anyway.
  2. "Install blocked" / "Unknown sources" — go to Settings → Security (or Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps), find Chrome / Files, and toggle Allow from this source on.
  3. Google Play Protect — it may say "blocked by Play Protect". Tap More details → Install anyway.
  4. "App not installed" — the APK may be for a different architecture. Make sure you downloaded the correct version for your device.
Chrome / other browsers (desktop)
  1. Chrome blocked the download — click the arrow or Show more next to the warning, then select Keep or Keep anyway.
  2. Download entirely blocked — press Ctrl+J to open downloads, find the file, and click Keep or Recover.
  3. Firefox / other — Look for the download arrow in the toolbar, click it, and select Allow or Keep.
Windows says suspicious?
  1. Windows Defender SmartScreen — "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then click Run anyway.
  2. Edge — Click the ... menu on the download and select Keep.
macOS says "damaged", "modified", or won't open?

This app isn't signed with an Apple Developer certificate, so macOS blocks it. First, open the DMG and drag the app into your Applications folder, then try these in order:

  1. Right-click → Open on the app in Applications (not double-click), then click Open in the dialog.
  2. Open Terminal and remove the quarantine flag from the DMG: 
    xattr -d com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/LocalFirst-Flashcards_0.3.3_aarch64.dmg
    Then open the DMG and drag the app to Applications again.
  3. Clear all extended attributes on the installed app: 
    xattr -cr /Applications/LocalFirst-Flashcards.app
  4. If you still see "app has been modified or damaged", fully reset Gatekeeper: 
    sudo xattr -rd com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LocalFirst-Flashcards.app
    You'll be prompted for your Mac login password.

Windows Defender or macOS Gatekeeper may flag unsigned apps. I build and scan every release myself — it's clean. Follow the steps above to override. Download and use at your own risk, if you have any strong, concrete proof it is this app that has caused damage or harm to your system I wil take legal responsiblity.
